In 2026, building smart AI isn't enough anymore – it also has to be secure and compliant. With regulations like the EU AI Act now in full effect, security can no longer be an afterthought. But how do you meet strict legal rules without slowing down your developers?

In this session, we will talk about both legal aspects and architecture. We will look at major AI vulnerabilities like prompt injection and data leaks, and map out a practical, 3-layer security framework using Google Cloud. You'll learn how to block threats with Cloud Armor, filter AI inputs and outputs in real time using Model Armor, and protect user privacy with Sensitive Data Protection. Join to find out how to build a complete protective shell around your AI models.
