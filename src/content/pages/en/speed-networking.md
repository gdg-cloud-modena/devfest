# Speed networking

Speed networking is an activity designed to spark quick, focused meetings between attendees.

Participants rotate through short one-to-one conversations — 3 minutes each — to introduce themselves, exchange contacts and spot possible professional synergies.

It's a dynamic and engaging format that lets you:

- quickly grow your network;
- get in touch with people who would otherwise be hard to reach;
- create collaboration opportunities in very little time.

The activity takes place in [Room 40](/en/2026/rooms/40/).
