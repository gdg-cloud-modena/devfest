## What is DevFest

DevFest is an event dedicated to developers, hosted by Google Developer Groups all around the world.

The Modena edition is built by the joint effort of the local communities: GDG Cloud Modena, Modena Full Stack, Reggio Emilia Full Stack, Flutter Modena, Conoscere Linux, ML Modena.

During the day you can join the [speed networking](/en/speed-networking/) to grow your professional network.

There is also room to propose your own [lightning talk](/en/2025/sessions/lightning-talk/).

Browse all the [sessions](/en/2026/sessions/) on the agenda.
