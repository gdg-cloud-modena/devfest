description: Learn by getting your hands dirty.
---

Workshops have a limited number of seats, roughly 20–25 depending on the session.

A few days before the event we will email a registration form to the address you used for your ticket.

On the form you can choose which workshops to attend, in order of priority.

At the event, please reach the room 10 minutes before the start. We will first let in those who expressed a preference, by time and priority, then the remaining people waiting until all seats are filled.
