The Sala Chiesa has its own external entrance beside the main doorway.

The seventeenth-century Church of San Carlo was commissioned by the Congregation
of the Blessed Virgin and Saint Charles from Roman architect Bartolomeo Avanzini.

Architect and stage designer Gaspare Vigarani refined the project, and
construction began in 1664 under master builder Giovan Pietro Piazza. The
decoration and dome were completed in the second half of the eighteenth
century. The marble altars, enriched by paintings from artists who also worked
for the ducal court, were funded through private bequests from the 1660s onward
and were not completed until the nineteenth century.

Capacity: 400 seated and 90 standing.
