Building a scalable design system can feel daunting when balancing design consistency, developer ergonomics, and business velocity.

In this session, we will explore a pragmatic approach based on Atomic Design principles to organize components, tokens, and documentation. We'll share battle-tested tips to bridge design and engineering, avoid common pitfalls, and maintain a living system that teams actually love using.
