Who is this workshop for?
Are you feeling at a crossroads in your career? Do you want to bring structure to your ideas, discover how to leverage your unique value, or explore new professional opportunities with a clear method?

This workshop is designed for developers, tech professionals, freelancers, and anyone wanting to consciously shape their professional path.

What is the Work Model Canvas?
Adapted from the famous Business Model Canvas, the Work Model Canvas is a visual tool that allows you to analyze yourself as a "one-person enterprise". You will map your skills, key partners, value proposition, and the resources needed to achieve your goals.

What will we do together?
- Analyze your current professional model
- Identify strengths and areas for growth
- Define a concrete action plan for your professional evolution
