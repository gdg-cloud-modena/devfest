Angular has undergone a complete renaissance. With Signals, new control flow syntax, standalone components, SSR hydration improvements, and Vite-powered builds, modern Angular is simpler, faster, and more developer-friendly than ever.

In this session, we'll explore all the major innovations, comparing old patterns with modern best practices, and showing why Angular is once again a top contender for enterprise web development.
