Reducing the gender gap in STEM and digital disciplines is essential for building a fairer, more innovative society.

This session presents the initiatives, projects, and public policies promoted by Regione Emilia-Romagna and Lepida to bridge the digital gender gap, support digital skills development, and foster inclusive participation across the territory.
