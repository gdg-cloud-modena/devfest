Digital sustainability is no longer an afterthought. Every byte transmitted, every server cycle executed, and every unoptimized asset contributes to the carbon footprint of the digital ecosystem.

In this talk, we explore practical tools and open-source strategies to measure and reduce the environmental impact of web applications. From performance optimizations and green hosting to efficient architectures and lightweight frameworks, learn how to build high-performance web products that respect the planet.
