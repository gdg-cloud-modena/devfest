Beyond algorithms, benchmarks, and model architectures lies the deepest dimension of the AI revolution: its impact on human emotions, empathy, relationships, and well-being.

This talk takes a psychological and relational look at how AI is transforming our daily lives, work dynamics, and interactions, asking the question: how do we preserve our humanity while embracing technological advancement?
