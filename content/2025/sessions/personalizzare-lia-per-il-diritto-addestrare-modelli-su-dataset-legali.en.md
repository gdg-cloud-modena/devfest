General-purpose LLMs often struggle with the nuances, precision, and jurisdictional specifics of legal language.

This session dives into the methodology of customizing AI models for legal applications: dataset curation, privacy and confidentiality compliance, domain-specific fine-tuning, and RAG architectures tailored to statutes, case law, and contracts.
