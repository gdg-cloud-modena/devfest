What if you could query BigQuery without writing complex SQL, getting clear and immediately usable results with minimal effort?

BigQuery Data Canvas is the tool you didn't know you needed: an intuitive, visual approach to explore, analyze, and transform data in just a few clicks.

In this talk, we'll see how it works, when to use it, and how it can simplify the daily workflow of developers and data enthusiasts who want maximum results with minimum friction.
