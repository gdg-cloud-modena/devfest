Modern distributed architectures make it challenging to track request flows across dozens of microservices.

In this talk, we explore the W3C Trace Context standard: what it is, how it works, and how it enables seamless distributed tracing across heterogeneous platforms, libraries, and cloud providers.

We will examine practical examples of propagation headers (`traceparent`, `tracestate`) and how OpenTelemetry leverages them to give full visibility into distributed systems.
