In this 2-hour workshop, we will start from scratch to build a modern Angular application leveraging the new Signal APIs designed to boost performance and simplify state management.

Say goodbye to Zone.js and discover Signals, the new Control Flow, and other modern APIs revolutionizing Angular!

A blend of theory and practice with live coding, real-world examples, and best practices.

What you will learn:
✅ How the new Signals reactivity model works
✅ How to create, read, and update Signals
✅ How to use Computed Signals and Effects
✅ How to manage application state with Signals
✅ How to write cleaner templates with new Control Flow (@if, @for, @switch)
✅ Best practices for simple, reactive, and efficient components
