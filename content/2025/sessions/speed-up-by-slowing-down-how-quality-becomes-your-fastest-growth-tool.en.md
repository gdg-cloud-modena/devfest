What if a lack of attention to software quality were the very thing slowing you down? In this talk, I will show how I introduced quality-oriented practices within agile teams even without a dedicated QA department, using business-friendly metrics and scalable strategies. This session is for developers, testers, and leaders who want to reduce risk, build confidence, and turn quality into an engine for growth.
