In modern cloud architectures, monitoring is not enough. You need true observability: the ability to understand internal system states from their external outputs.

This session covers the core pillars of observability—metrics, logs, and distributed traces—and how to implement effective telemetry using open standards like OpenTelemetry. Learn how to diagnose issues faster, reduce MTTR, and maintain high reliability.
