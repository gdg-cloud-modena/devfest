DevPunks is a collective of freelance full-stack developers based in Turin, Milan, Lugano, and Sofia. We bring experienced professionals together in flexible, coordinated teams to support companies as their technology needs grow.
Our Team-as-a-Service model creates tailored teams by selecting the developers in our community who best match each client's needs.

We assess more than technical skills: we also consider experience, seniority, and the ability to integrate into an existing organisation. This lets us provide qualified people quickly while reducing the time and complexity of recruitment.
We specialise in frontend and mobile development, backend systems, and Python-based data and AI solutions.

Our talent network covers a broad range of skills for projects spanning digital products, enterprise applications, and data-driven initiatives.
DevPunks' real value lies in its community. Our strong, carefully selected network lets us engage excellent freelancers quickly, combining flexibility and reliability without compromising delivery quality. We are not simply a supplier: we are a technical partner that helps clients scale their technology and product teams sustainably during periods of rapid growth.

With DevPunks, companies and scale-ups gain a single point of contact, immediate access to specialist expertise, and a lean collaboration model that makes it easier to turn ideas into products and measurable results.
