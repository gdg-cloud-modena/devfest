ADT Group is a European ecosystem headquartered in Modena, orchestrating human and digital evolution through digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and cloud-native solutions — delivered by specialized companies under a single accountability.

Netmetrix Group, with direct presence beyond Italy in Madrid, Lisbon, Paris, London and Dubai, makes digital infrastructures observable, reliable and manageable, from the lab through to live production. Within it, Cloudmetrix offers solutions for cloud management, data and business applications. K Labs delivers technical and vendor certification training, People Labs works on personal and organisational growth.

We built the group this way because AI has changed what failure looks like. A system that answers can be corrected; a system that acts has already moved the record and sent the instruction. Verification stops being a quality concern and becomes a question of accountability — and the people supervising it have to be ready.

Within ADT Group, we assure digital trust and drive people evolution.
