Active since 2000 with extensive experience in the IT sector, Quix is a Modena-based software house that puts technology at the service of business. As a strategic partner for digital transformation, it supports companies with tailored solutions based on Cloud, Artificial Intelligence, Data Platforms, the Salesforce ecosystem, and B2B e-commerce. A dynamic organization focused on continuous innovation and process optimization, Quix joined the Horsa Group in 2026 to amplify its synergies and impact across the IT landscape.
