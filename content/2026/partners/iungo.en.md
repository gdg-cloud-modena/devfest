For 25 years, IUNGO spa Società Benefit has been providing solutions to enhance Supply Chain Collaboration through the excellence of its innovative IUNGO software solution.

Originating as a spin-off from the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, IUNGO holds 3 international patents, serves 415 clients, and connects 150,000 active suppliers across 44 countries worldwide.
