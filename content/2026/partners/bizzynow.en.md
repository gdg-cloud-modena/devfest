Bizzy S.r.l. is an Italian startup created with the goal of making professional networking simpler, more effective, and natural, transforming events, trade fairs, and communities into concrete opportunities to build new relationships and business opportunities.

The BizzyNow platform enables professionals to discover and meet the most relevant people within the same setting, facilitating real-time connections based on shared interests and goals. Through verified professional profiles, targeted matching, and integrated communication tools, BizzyNow brings digital networking into the real world, helping people connect in the right place at the right time.

Our vision stems from a simple principle: attending the same event does not necessarily mean meeting the right people. That is why we built a tool that turns often passive attendance into an active experience, fostering spontaneous encounters and high-value professional connections.

BizzyNow is not limited to the duration of a single event. Connections and communities built on the platform can continue over time, enabling professionals, companies, and organizers to keep relationships active and develop new opportunities long after the physical event concludes.
