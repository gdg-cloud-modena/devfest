Based on (more than) one true story: how can we survive as tech workers in 2026? What challenges do we encounter day by day? What are the risks? What strategies can we adopt to manage our daily routines?

We will address these and other topics from two complementary perspectives: that of a nerd (myself) and that of a psychotherapist (Dr. Marika Silvestri, psychologist and psychotherapist).
