Artificial intelligence is redefining how software is designed, understood, and maintained. We are no longer just talking about autocomplete or chatbots inside IDEs: the industry is entering the era of AI-Augmented Development, where intelligent agents actively collaborate with developers to analyze codebases, generate documentation, explain architectures, and accelerate debugging and refactoring tasks.

This talk will explore how tools like CodeWiki, MCP, Skills, and other modern AI platforms are transforming Developer Experience through increasingly intelligent, conversational workflows.

A live practical demo will show how to use AI to comprehend large repositories, generate automatic knowledge bases, query code using natural language, and boost onboarding and team productivity.

The session is designed for developers, tech leads, and innovators eager to understand how AI Agents are reshaping the future of software engineering.
