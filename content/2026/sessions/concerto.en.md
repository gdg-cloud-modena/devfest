To close the first day of DevFest Modena 2026, the stage of the historic **Teatro della Fondazione San Carlo** lights up with an exclusive **Acoustic Live** concert by **Violet Blend**, an award-winning alternative metal band from Florence.

In an intimate and powerful acoustic arrangement showcasing extraordinary lead vocals, nuanced piano textures, and rhythmic drive, Violet Blend will perform reimagined tracks from their acclaimed albums *Demons*, *White Mask*, *Live and True*, and their latest releases.

Their music pairs expressive power with deep social commitment, from campaigns against gender-based violence (*La Donna Mobile*) to anti-stalking awareness initiatives like the educational card game *Talk or Stalk?*.

An unmissable plenary evening for the entire community of developers, speakers, and tech enthusiasts to connect, enjoy live music, and network in a spectacular venue!

Lineup:
- **Giada Celeste Chelli** &ndash; Lead Vocals & Piano
- **Ferruccio Baroni** &ndash; Acoustic Bass & Backing Vocals
- **Michel Agostini** &ndash; Percussion & Backing Vocals

Learn more about the band on their [official website](https://www.violetblend.cloud/) and on the [Violet Blend dedicated profile](/speakers/violet-blend/).
