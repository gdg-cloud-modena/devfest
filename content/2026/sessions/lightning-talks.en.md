## How to propose your lightning talk {#how}

Submit your talk in the "Lightning talk" channel on [Telegram](https://t.me/devfest_modena), including its **title**, your **first and last name**, and a short **description**.

Selected talks will be announced at lunchtime on Saturday, again on Telegram, and will appear on this page.

Speakers should arrive shortly before the session starts so they have time to prepare.

Please note: the ten-minute limit is strict 😉 and promotional talks are not accepted.
