AI is changing software engineering, but perhaps not in the way we first expected. It is not simply replacing tasks or accelerating workflows. It is exposing what has always mattered most in great engineering work.

When writing code becomes easier, the harder questions become more important: What are we really trying to solve? What should we trust? What deserves more scrutiny? What makes a solution not just functional, but good? As AI becomes part of the everyday engineering workflow, the value of an engineer increasingly shows up in the decisions around the code, not just in the code itself.

This talk is an optimistic reflection on how the role of the software engineer is evolving, and on what becomes easier to recognize when the tools get more powerful. We’ll look at what AI changes, what it reveals, and why the qualities that make engineers truly effective are becoming more visible, not less important.

Attendees will leave with a more grounded view of the future of engineering, one where AI changes the mechanics of the craft, but makes human judgment, ownership, and intent more important than ever.
