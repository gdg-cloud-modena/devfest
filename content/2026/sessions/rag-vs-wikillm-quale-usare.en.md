Everyone is talking about RAG, but is it always the best choice? In this lightning talk, we explore the differences between RAG and WikiLLM, two approaches we use at NetAI to build AI solutions powered by company knowledge.

Through concrete use cases, we compare advantages, limitations, and trade-offs of both approaches to determine when a RAG pipeline is genuinely needed and when structuring and optimizing knowledge for LLMs is more effective. Ten minutes to answer a very practical question: RAG or WikiLLM, which one should you choose for your project?
