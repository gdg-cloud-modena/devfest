What is the impact of Agentic AI transformation on people and organizations?

This session examines what actually shifts when execution moves to an AI agent: the unit of work, the shape of supervision, where accountability sits, and what seniority means.

It works through four key questions that decide the outcome:
- Who will actually delegate tasks to AI agents?
- Who will be destabilised by the change?
- What does daily work actually become?
- Will the organisation proactively design the change or just absorb it?

It will also present a practical working methodology to map, assess, and manage agentic transformation projects from both a people and an organizational perspective.
