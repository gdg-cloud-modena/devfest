Choosing to attend DevFest this weekend already says something about you: ambition is not an option, but your standard. Excellence is not a possibility, but the only path forward.

Top performers who generate a significant impact in their professional environment achieve outstanding results because that level of performance has become their baseline normal.

In tech, and even more so for those from underrepresented groups, such high standards can become a coping strategy to feel worthy and earn hard-won recognition from others.

Yet behavioral research tells us something uncomfortable. The very same patterns that produce exceptional performance—such as identity heavily tied to outcomes, extraordinarily high excellence standards, and discomfort with imperfection—are linked to an invisible plateau: it's not that you perform less, but you stop taking risks. And in doing so, you stop growing.

This talk does not question the excellence that brought us here. On the contrary, it explores it to understand where it continues to open doors and where, instead, it risks becoming a ceiling. What would happen if you gave yourself permission to embrace imperfection to reach your next level?
