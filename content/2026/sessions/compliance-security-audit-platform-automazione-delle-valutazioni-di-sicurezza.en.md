Nowadays, proper data and environment management is essential; business forecasts, budgets, decision-making strategies, and much more depend on them. Managing security and regulatory compliance is a concrete priority for any modern organization. Configuration and usage are becoming increasingly complex, and proper governance is indispensable for business forecasting. It is fundamental to guarantee regulatory standards and security best practices to protect corporate value.

The problem is concrete: data centers and cloud accounts often contain poorly managed sensitive data, vulnerable configurations resulting from rushed business developments, and compliance gaps that create weaknesses for potential attacks. Identifying and resolving these issues requires significant resources and considerable time, making it impossible for many organizations to maintain an up-to-date view of the security posture of their environments.

For this reason, we developed a platform that automates the entire assessment process. We monitor SQL Server environments both on-premises and in the cloud (AWS and Azure), performing in-depth analyses of system configurations and identifying regulatory gaps. The platform compares the current state of infrastructure with recognized compliance standards such as CIS, DORA, NIS2, and GDPR, generating a detailed analysis. Artificial intelligence is utilized to automate part of the process.

The goal is to provide users, via a web interface, with an advanced assessment of system configurations, running dynamic checks on regulatory gaps, and benchmarking infrastructure against compliance standards (CIS, DORA, NIS2, GDPR, and sector-specific best practices). The system produces detailed findings with severity levels, categorization, and technical context, allowing tracking of the status of every identified issue.

In summary, we transform long and complex security assessments into an automated, consistent, and timely process.
