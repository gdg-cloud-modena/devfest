Discover how artificial intelligence comes to life by building ideas with LEGO in a practical, creative, and engaging workshop!

An experiential workshop using the **LEGO® Serious Play®** facilitation methodology to make tangible and shareable the representations and **implications of AI in daily work activities**.

Through hands-on building and facilitated dialogue, participants will explore applications, opportunities, and key considerations of artificial intelligence within their operational contexts.

**Facilitator:**
**Roberto Ceschina** – Trainer & Coach, certified LEGO® SERIOUS PLAY® facilitator. Coach and trainer specialized in leadership and decision-making development through experiential methodologies.
