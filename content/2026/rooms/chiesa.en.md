Access to the Church room is from outside, right next to the main entrance.

The 17th-century Church of San Carlo was commissioned by the Congregation to Roman architect Bartolomeo Avanzini. The interior offers magnificent acoustics and architecture.

Capacity: 400 seated + 90 standing.
