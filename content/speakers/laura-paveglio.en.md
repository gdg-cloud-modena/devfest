I am a freelance UX/UI Designer, with roots in industrial design and grown within the fantastic digital world.
I live and breathe design. I love beauty, especially when it is functional.

With over 12 years of experience, I bring unique value to the design of intuitive and accessible digital experiences.
I have had the opportunity to apply my skills across a wide range of projects and industries, including fintech, automotive, tech, publishing, sports, events, and tourism to create interfaces that are both aesthetically pleasing and functional.

I lead and collaborate with cross-functional teams to transform research and strategy into practical, tangible solutions. I am also a trainer in UX/UI Design courses, as well as a 1:1 coach and mentor for junior designers, supporting their professional growth and skills development. Through my blog "Read the f*cking manual", I share knowledge and innovations in design.
