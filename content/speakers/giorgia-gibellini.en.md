I am a UX Designer & Researcher working on complex systems within industrial packaging machinery.

Within a team primarily composed of developers, I built from scratch the practices, user flows, and interfaces that now guide product development, testing, and documentation.

I come from a technical background in computer science, but chose to dedicate myself to experience design, focusing on the intersection between humans and technology—which is what truly drives my passion.

Outside work, I love contributing to and growing communities: I organize and participate in hackathons, serve as a SheTech Ambassador in Modena, and founded UXEvents, a hub dedicated to promoting soft tech events and culture. I am passionate about strategic foresight, AI design, gamification, and ethics in technology.
