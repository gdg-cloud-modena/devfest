Giacomo Sergio has 25 years of experience in the IT industry. After earning his degree in Engineering and gaining research experience at Consorzio Pisa Ricerche, he moved to Vienna to work as a software developer in the financial sector. In 2008, he founded Wondersys. Under his leadership, Wondersys has grown to over 20 employees, delivering innovative IT services and solutions both in Italy and internationally. Passionate about AI, he actively champions its adoption across the software applications delivered by the company.
