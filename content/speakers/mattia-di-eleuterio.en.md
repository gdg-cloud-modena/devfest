Mattia is a Software Engineer at QubicaAMF Worldwide, where he works on enterprise software and cloud-based solutions. Passionate about backend systems, distributed applications, and infrastructure, he is also an active open source developer and self-hosting enthusiast.

Mattia is the creator of Vod2Pod-RSS, a popular open source project written in Rust with nearly 400 GitHub stars, and his work has been featured on the Linux Unplugged podcast. He enjoys sharing practical experiences from building scalable systems, automation, and modern developer tooling.

Outside of technology, he organizes a weekly international meetup in Bologna that brings together locals, expats, and travelers, helping foster a vibrant and welcoming community.
