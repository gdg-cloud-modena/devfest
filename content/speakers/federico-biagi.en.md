Federico Biagi is a computer engineer and PhD candidate at the "Enzo Ferrari" Department of Engineering at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia (UNIMORE). For the past two years, he has been working in the field of Assistive Robotics, aiming to integrate Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision into the development of robotic frameworks designed to actively support people in care and domestic environments.
