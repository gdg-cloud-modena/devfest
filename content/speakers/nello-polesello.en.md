Nello Polesello graduated in Computer Science from the University of Udine and is a co-founder of infoFACTORY srl, a university spinoff company specialising in advanced web technologies. With over 20 years of experience in the IT sector, he designs and builds websites, portals, and web applications from requirement analysis to post-release maintenance.

He is a full-stack developer who has built prominent projects for both public institutions and private companies, using Python on the backend and modern frontend technologies.

Alongside his entrepreneurial activities, he has taught numerous IFTS courses, primarily focusing on Python, Django, and mobile web development.

In recent years he has focused on web apps and PWAs, exploring lesser-known web capabilities to build creative and unconventional solutions.
