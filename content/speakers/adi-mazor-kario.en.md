With over 20 years in the tech industry, Adi is an expert at the intersection of AI, tech startups, and entrepreneurship. Her work is centered on helping startups build successful products and grow, always maintaining a deep focus on the humans behind the users.

Expertise & Impact:
Driving Startup Success: For 15 years, Adi has served as a Google Developer Expert (GDE) in AI and a Google Accelerator Mentor, where she guides founders in transforming complex AI capabilities into scalable, market-ready products.

Teaching the Next Generation: At Reichman University’s Entrepreneurship School, she teaches Product and AI, dedicated to helping the next generation of entrepreneurs build the skills they need to launch and lead in the tech world.

Humanizing the Tech Space: Through her podcast, "AI & Humans" (Spotify/YouTube), she explores how technology can better serve people, a philosophy she brings to every product she touches.

A Multi-Faceted Life: Adi balances her tech-driven career with a busy home life—she is a mother of four kids, four cats, and a dog, keeping her grounded in the real world.

The Big Picture: Adi combines two decades of high-level experience in AI and product design to help startups scale efficiently while keeping their products human-centered and impactful.
