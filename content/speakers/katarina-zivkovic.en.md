Kat Zivkovic is a brand & content strategist with a strong passion for storytelling, strategy, and digital transformation. As Managing Editor at Sysdig, she leads strategies across blogs, video, and email to elevate brand awareness in the cybersecurity industry. With a background spanning publishing, multi-channel marketing, and international team leadership, she helps companies and professionals craft impactful narratives.

Kat is fluent in five languages, holds a Master's degree from Humboldt University of Berlin, and serves as DEI Council Chair at Sysdig, championing inclusion across the tech sector.

She firmly believes that the right story, told the right way, has the power to open doors and transform careers.
