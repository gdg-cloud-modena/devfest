His adventure began with an Intel 80386, and he graduated in Computer Science from the University of Turin, followed by two Master's degrees: one in Digital Communication / Web Marketing and Social Media Management, and a second in Cloud Computing.

Following roles as a web and mobile developer, he is now a Cloud Architect, university lecturer and teaching assistant, and co-organizer of droidcon Italy, Swift Heroes, and Flutter Heroes.

On the community side, he founded GDG Cloud Torino in 2018, became a Google Champion Innovator in 2023, and a Google GDE in Cloud in 2025.

On Instagram, he is known as @mosquitoman81, one of the most followed Italian creators covering board games, gaming, and fairs.
