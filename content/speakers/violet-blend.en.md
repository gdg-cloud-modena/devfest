**Violet Blend** are an Italian alternative metal band founded in Florence, Italy, featuring extraordinary vocals, rich sonic power, and unbridled energy. The band lineup is:

- **Giada Celeste Chelli** &ndash; Lead vocals, piano & orchestration
- **Ferruccio Baroni** &ndash; Bass & backing vocals
- **Michel Agostini** &ndash; Drums & backing vocals

### Discography & Music
- **White Mask** (2018) &ndash; Debut record exposing the fallacy of using social media to represent the self (*"Burn your mask"*), accompanied by a striking guerrilla art campaign in Florence with more than 5,000 white masks bearing a purple 'V'.
- **Demons** (2022) &ndash; Released worldwide via Eclipse Records, exploring internal masks across 13 tracks illustrated by demonic masks from world mythologies. Alongside the album, the band launched *La Donna Mobile*, a non-profit campaign against gender violence and discrimination in collaboration with AIDOS and the Municipality of Florence.
- **Live and True** (2023) &ndash; The band's first live album, capturing the raw power and authentic emotion of their live performances.
- **The Reign of Sadness** (2025) &ndash; Single and video leading an anti-stalking awareness campaign, which also inspired **Talk or Stalk?**, an educational card game designed alongside psychology experts to detect early red flags of toxic behavior.

### Touring & Accolades
Violet Blend has performed across Europe at major festivals such as I-Days Festival (supporting Radiohead), Loverocks Festival (UK), Wildfire Festival (Scotland), and Eber-Hart Festival (Germany), headlining and supporting renowned acts like Garbage, Palaye Royale, Chris Slade (AC/DC), Vinnie Moore (Alice Cooper), and Hanabie.

Among their accolades: *Band of the Year 2023* (Great Music Stories, UK), *Best Band of the Year 2022* (Metal Liar, USA), *Best Rock Band 2022* (Vox Platinum Radio, Mexico), *Best Album of the Year 2022* (Heavy Metal Heaven, Italy), and the *#togetherforthemusic 2020* award.
