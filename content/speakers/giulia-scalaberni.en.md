Giulia started her career as a web developer, evolved into a data analyst, and is now a Product Manager for a BI tool at an IT company in the healthcare sector. She leads remote Scrum teams composed of members from 8 different cultures, including India. She is relentlessly people-centered, fascinated by the way diverse cultures interact. Always eager to learn and explore cultural nuances, she works to make teamwork more "human" and mindful of cognitive biases. When she isn't shaping product strategies or reflecting on team dynamics, you can find her at concerts imagining behind-the-scenes tour management, or with a flight ticket already booked: Osaka and Lisbon are her favorite cities. She is hopelessly obsessed with bubble tea, loves kangaroos, and shares practical insights that anyone can try out with their team the very next day.
