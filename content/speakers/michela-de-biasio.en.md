Born in Pordenone in 1988, she graduated with honors in Political Science from the University of Padua and earned a PhD in Spatial Planning and Public Policy from IUAV University of Venice. During her studies and research work, she developed a deep passion for social, urban, and technological innovation, combining it with her lifelong love of writing. This fusion gave rise to a hybrid, continuously evolving career, leading her to work both as a freelance writer and as a Communication Manager and Content Creator for diverse physical and digital organizations. After working at the Regional Department for the Digital Agenda of Emilia-Romagna, she is now embarking on a new journey at Lepida, where she passionately manages projects related to digital skills, bridging the Digital Gender Gap, Citizen Science, and organizing events in these domains.
