Co-founder and CEO of ADT Group. Born in 1967 and holding a degree in Physics, he has over 30 years of experience in the Information Technology sector. He is currently co-founder and CEO of Advanced Digital Technologies, an industrial holding company operating through group companies in several European countries. The group specializes in the design and management of IT infrastructures, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, application development and data analysis solutions, as well as technical and management training and consulting.
