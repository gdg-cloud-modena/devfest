# Workshops

Workshops have limited capacity, approximately 20-25 seats per session.

We will send an email registration form a few days prior to the event to the email address used when booking your ticket.

On the form, you can select which workshops you'd like to attend and set your priority.

On the day of the event, please arrive at the room 10 minutes before the session begins. Registered attendees will be admitted first based on registration timestamp and priority, followed by waiting attendees until capacity is reached.
