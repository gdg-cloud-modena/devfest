{{/* Resolve the room pages a session takes place in, in the order listed on the session. Unknown room codes are skipped rather than rendered as broken links. Callers need the count as well as the pages (one room reads "in room X", more than one reads "in parallel in X, Y"), so this returns pages rather than pre-rendered markup. @param {string} year Edition, e.g. "2026" @param {[]string} codes Room codes from a session's "rooms" front matter @return {[]page.Page} */}} {{- $rooms := partialCached "lookup/rooms" .year .year -}} {{- $found := slice -}} {{- range .codes -}} {{- with index $rooms . -}} {{- $found = $found | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $found -}}
